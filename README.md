# 🎭 Kalavidara-Balaga  
**Android App Development using GenAI – Employment Project**

---

## 📌 Overview  
Kalavidara-Balaga is an Android application designed to support traditional folk artists by providing a digital platform to showcase their talent and connect with event managers. The app helps bridge the gap between rural artists and urban opportunities, making traditional art a sustainable profession.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement  
Local folk artists such as Dollu Kunitha and Pooja Kunitha troupes often struggle to find work outside festival and wedding seasons. Due to lack of digital presence, they are unable to reach event planners who are actively looking for authentic performers.

---

## 💡 Solution  
This application acts as a **Folk Artist Talent Hub**, where artists can:
- Register themselves  
- Create performance profiles  
- Upload images (portfolio/gallery)  
- Provide contact details  

Event organizers can:
- Browse artist listings  
- Filter based on district and art type  
- View detailed profiles  
- Directly contact artists  

---

## 🚀 Features  

### 👤 Artist Management
- Add new artist profiles  
- View artist details  
- Real-time data from Firebase  

### 🔍 Search & Filter
- Filter by **District**  
- Filter by **Art Type**  

### 🖼️ Gallery
- Display multiple images  
- Click to view in full screen  

### 📞 Booking Feature
- Direct call option using phone dialer  

### ⭐ Favorites
- Mark/unmark artists as favorite  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack  

- **Language:** Kotlin  
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose  
- **Database:** Firebase Firestore  
- **Image Loading:** Coil  
- **IDE:** Android Studio
